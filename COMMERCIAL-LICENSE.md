## Overview

**Social AI** is dual-licensed:
- **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** for open source use
- **Commercial License** for proprietary use or large-scale deployments

## Free Use Under AGPLv3

You can use, study, modify, and distribute Social AI freely under AGPLv3 if you:
- Keep your modifications open source (AGPLv3 requires this)
- Provide source code to users of your network service
- Comply with all AGPLv3 terms

This is free for:
- Personal projects
- Academic research
- Non-profits
- Small community deployments
- Any organization that can comply with AGPLv3 copyleft requirements

## When You Need a Commercial License

A commercial license is required if:

### 1. Large-Scale Deployment
Your deployment serves **more than 1,000 active users** and you cannot or prefer not to open source your implementation.

### 2. Proprietary Modifications
You want to:
- Modify the software without releasing changes as open source
- Build proprietary features on top of Social AI
- Integrate with proprietary systems without open sourcing them

### 3. Commercial Service
You want to:
- Offer Social AI as a paid service without AGPLv3 obligations
- Include it in a commercial product
- Use it in enterprise environments that cannot accept copyleft licenses

### 4. Patents and Warranties
Your organization requires:
- Patent indemnification
- Commercial warranties and support
- Service level agreements (SLAs)

## Why Dual Licensing?

This model ensures:
- **Accessibility:** Small projects, researchers, and communities can use Social AI freely
- **Sustainability:** Large commercial deployments contribute to ongoing development
- **Openness:** All improvements under AGPLv3 benefit everyone
- **Fairness:** Organizations that cannot open source their work can still participate

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use Social AI for free in my startup?
Yes, if you comply with AGPLv3 (open source your modifications) and serve fewer than 1,000 users.

### What counts as an "active user"?
A person who interacts with the system at least once per month.

### Can I evaluate Social AI before deciding on licensing?
Yes! Use it under AGPLv3 for evaluation. Contact us when you're ready to deploy at scale.

### What if I'm a non-profit serving 2,000 users?
Contact us. We're interested in supporting community use and can discuss terms.

### Can I contribute code under a commercial license?
All contributions are accepted under AGPLv3. Contributors retain copyright but license their work to the project under AGPLv3 terms.
